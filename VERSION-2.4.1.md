# WageTrack 2.4.1

- Future shift starts automatically choose Planned, including calendar and template entry. Manual status choices survive time/date edits, with a button to return to automatic selection. Mixed batch dates are evaluated individually.
- Cancelled status with employer/me/unspecified reason, retained calendar and list history and a monthly cancellation summary. Excluded from payroll, forecasts, reminders and paid CSV totals.
- Settings: Auto-complete planned shifts (enabled by default). Completes after scheduled end in local time, handles overnight shifts, and catches up on launch/resume. No background service is required. Running clocks protect overlapping plans.
- The clock links a unique planned shift at its workplace when started during the shift or up to 30 minutes early. Finishing updates that record with actual time and retains its original schedule; cancelled records are never resurrected.
- Ongoing / On break badge at the top-right of the clock.

Permanent application ID, signing key and existing storage retained.
