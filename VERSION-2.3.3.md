# WageTrack 2.3.3

Reminders now use a compact list with grouped rows and switches, inspired by the supplied reference.

- Upcoming-shift and logging reminders toggle on or off immediately without a separate Save action.
- Timing, scope, weekday and time settings open in focused dialogs with Save and Back/Close to cancel.
- The home page shows short summaries; notification diagnostics, tests, rescheduling and background guidance are under Notification help.
- Native status refreshes preserve an open settings draft.
- The blue app accent, English/German translations and light/dark themes are retained.

This release changes the reminder interface and settings interactions. It does not change native notification delivery or claim to resolve the previously reported device-specific delivery issue.

Validation covers both one-tap switches, native bridge persistence, hours/days and selected-shift settings, weekday validation, cancel/draft behavior, compact mobile layouts and existing notification tests.
