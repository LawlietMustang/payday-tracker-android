# Version 2.2.0

## Workplace fix (2.1.1)

Both workplace selectors now have visible New workplace and Manage workplaces buttons. The management page offers Edit, Archive/Restore, and Delete. Creating a workplace from the shift form selects it immediately and preserves the draft. Wage changes apply to new shifts; recorded wages stay unchanged.

Deleting requires an active destination for existing records and a confirmation. Shifts and payslips retain their values. Deletion is blocked for the last active workplace, an active timer's workplace, or a destination with a conflicting payslip month. Archive is available to preserve the original employer identity.

## Widget

Menu → Widget & security → Add widget, or use the launcher's widget picker. Shows Working, On break, or Ready to start. Android's chronometer updates elapsed work/break time while the app is backgrounded. Tap to open the app. This version does not start/finish shifts directly from the home screen. With app lock enabled, widget times are hidden. It does not show wages or personal expenses.

## Reminders

Choose a local time and weekdays for a recurring reminder to log hours. Reminders are off initially. Notifications require user permission; the settings screen reports blocked notifications and links to Android notification settings. The schedule is restored after a reboot, app update, or time-zone/clock change. These are inexact alarms: battery saving and Android may delay them. Force-stopping the app requires reopening it to resume scheduled work.

## App lock

Optional native biometric authentication, with phone PIN/pattern/password fallback. Android 8 uses device credentials; Android 9+ supports the native biometric prompt where available. A screen lock must already be configured. Enabling and disabling require authentication. The WebView is covered before loading when locked, hidden when leaving the app, and remains inaccessible after a canceled/failed attempt. Screenshots and recent-app previews are restricted while the lock is enabled. Background timers and reminders continue. This is an app-access gate, not additional encryption of the stored financial data.

## Build and validation

Version code 18. The validation workflow compiles a separate debug package without distributing it and checks workplace interactions, planning, and responsive layouts. Release distribution continues to require the permanent signing secrets documented in SIGNING.md. No replacement signing key is generated by CI.

Physical-device acceptance remains necessary: enroll biometrics and test success, failure, cancellation and PIN fallback; kill/relaunch the app; background/foreground it; add/resize the widget; run work and break timers; test reminders with permission denied/granted and after reboot/time-zone changes, including Xiaomi battery-saving modes. Browser tests cannot verify these Android behaviors.

Platform references: https://developer.android.com/reference/android/hardware/biometrics/BiometricPrompt.Builder , https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/appwidgets , https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms .
